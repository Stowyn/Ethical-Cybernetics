# Ethical-Cybernetics
A framework for relational, non‑consumptive systems. 
This project sits at the intersection of relational physics, information theory, and classical ethical philosophy, asking not whether the universe can be modeled, but what constraints relational reality places on meaning, responsibility, and action.

Scientific / Theoretical Foundations

Lee Smolin — Time Reborn; Smolin & Unger: relational and temporal naturalism, evolving laws, rejection of block-universe absolutism

Carlo Rovelli — Relational Quantum Mechanics; Loop Quantum Gravity and spin-foam models

Seth Lloyd — The universe as a quantum information processor

Max Tegmark — Mathematical Universe Hypothesis (reality as mathematical structure)

Giulio Tononi — Integrated Information Theory (Φ as intrinsic causal structure)

John Archibald Wheeler — “It from Bit” (information as fundamental)

Erik Verlinde — Emergent / entropic gravity

Karl Friston — Free-energy principle; information-theoretic self-organising systems

Philosophical / Ethical Lineage (Not Optional)

Baruch Spinoza — Monism; God/Nature as a single self-consistent system governed by necessity and relation

Marcus Aurelius (Stoicism) — Logos, duty without ego, action aligned with the whole rather than mastery over it

Fyodor Dostoevsky — Moral psychology, responsibility, the danger of purely rational systems divorced from compassion and lived meaning
